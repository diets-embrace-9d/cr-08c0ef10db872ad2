Spoken vs Written

Hong Kong speaks Cantonese but writes Standard Written Chinese (書面語), which follows Mandarin word choice and grammar. Signs, menus, newspapers, notices and subtitles are nearly all written that way — so the word on the page is often not the word you'd say out loud.

The key thing to know:

Written Chinese in Hong Kong is still read aloud in Cantonese. A newsreader seeing 是 says si6 — never the Mandarin shì — even though in conversation the same person would say 係 hai6. Every form and example sentence here is playable, so you can hear both the way a Hongkonger would say them.
